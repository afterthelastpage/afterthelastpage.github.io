About

Welcome to afterthelastpage — a small corner of the internet for essays, short stories, and the thoughts that live in the margins.

This is where I write about books, light, seasons, and the ordinary things that turn out to matter. It is named after the feeling you get when you close a book and the story hasn't quite left you yet.
