Personal Essay · No. 001

Unclaimed Platform

inspired by: "do you think our vows ever grieve the fact that they never made it to the aisle?"

We are two routes drawn on the same map, but stopped just before its line touched the cold altar.

"Could there be a silent sorrow lingering in our vows?"

We now stand still on a lonely platform, holding tickets to a destination whose departure date has been forcibly erased by circumstances. expelled by a travel schedule that no longerleaving a pair of shadows with no place to reside.

Two train cars side by side on parallel tracks gazing at each other from behind the glass, yet impossible to draw closer. moving quickly in different directions trapped in an unescapable momentum.

Now silence becomes the only remaining witness, tending to the remnants of a perished love that was never named.

We move away, dragging the lingering scent that grows fainter, leaving those promises as a masterpiece that will never be exhibited.

A ceremony that died before being solmenized.
