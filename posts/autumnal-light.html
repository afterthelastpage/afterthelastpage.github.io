Personal Essay · No. 001

Autumnal Light

On the particular quality of afternoon sun in October, and why it makes me feel like I am running out of time.

There is a specific hour in October when the light turns and everything becomes unbearably beautiful. It happens around four in the afternoon. The sun drops low enough to come in sideways — golden and thick — catching the dust in the air and the edges of leaves not yet fallen.

"I always feel, in October, that summer slipped away while I was looking at something else."

I have tried to explain this to people and I cannot do it well. It is not sadness, exactly. It is more like the awareness of time — the way you suddenly notice a clock only when it is close to stopping. October light is like that. It is the most beautiful light of the year precisely because it is almost gone.

I keep a notebook where I write down small things. This time last year I wrote: four pm, the wall, the way it looked like honey. I did not write anything else. I already knew what I meant.

Maybe that is all writing is, in the end. A way to say: I was here. The light was like this. I noticed.
